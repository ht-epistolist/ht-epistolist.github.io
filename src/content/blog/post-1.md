# The Intersection of Art and Engineering

It is often said that art and engineering are two sides of the same coin. I have spent the last few years exploring this boundary.

## Technical creativity
When we write code, we are composing a structure that must be both elegant and functional. Similarly, when creating a generative art piece, the algorithms dictate the flow of the visual narrative.

I believe that the best engineers are artists at heart, and the best artists possess an engineer's intuition.
